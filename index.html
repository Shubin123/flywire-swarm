Overlays

Each fly carries a heritable disposition toward the selfish option (Hawk / Defect / Hare). Whether it actually plays it also depends on the activity of octopaminergic (OA-) cell types in its own connectome — octopamine promotes aggression in flies (Hoyer et al. 2008; Zhou, Rao & Rao 2008). Every ~8 s a fly compares its recent payoff with another fly's and copies that fly's disposition with the Fermi probability (Traulsen, Nowak & Pacheco 2006), so the population follows replicator dynamics (Taylor & Jonker 1978) toward an evolutionarily stable state (Maynard Smith & Price 1973). Mutation and the finite swarm keep it near, not exactly on, the analytic line. Copying only from graph neighbours can shift the outcome (Hauert & Doebeli 2004).
